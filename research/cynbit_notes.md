## 8. Key Observations & Visible Gaps

### Confirmed facts:
- Cynbit *sells* AI agent development to clients but does not appear to use an AI agent on its *own* website for lead capture.
- Contact flow appears to rely on static forms → manual email triage.
- The blog ("The Architect's Digest") is the primary inbound/content channel.
- Team is ~15 people – every manual process (lead triage, proposal writing, client FAQ response) consumes outsized engineering time.
- No publicly accessible pricing or service tier pages (all leads go through direct contact).
- Products like BitElevate are AI-driven but no evidence of an AI assistant helping users navigate the learning platform.

### Inferences (clearly marked):
- *Inference:* Given ~15 person team and bootstrapped status, pre-sales qualification is likely done manually by founders/senior staff – a significant time tax.
- *Inference:* The gap between "what we sell" (AI agents) and "how we operate" (static contact form) is the highest-credibility opportunity – building it demonstrates dogfooding.
- *Inference:* Blog content exists but no RAG-based search or Q&A layer on top of it – readers cannot interactively query Cynbit's expertise.
- *Inference:* TenantEase (property management SaaS) likely has a support/FAQ surface area that could benefit from AI-powered customer support.
